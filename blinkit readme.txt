This project delivers a comprehensive performance analysis dashboard modeled after Blinkit's quick-commerce operations. By breaking down sales, product attributes, 
and outlet demographics, this business intelligence tool transforms raw retail data into actionable logistical insights.

### Key Executive Performance Metrics
* ** Total Sales Revenue:** $1.20M
* ** Average Ticket Size (Sales):** $141
* ** Active Inventory Count:** 8,523 unique items
* ** Consolidated Customer Rating:** 3.9 / 5.0

---

##  Key Business Insights Discovered

### 1. Channel Performance Matrix
* ** Supermarket Type 
1**  dominates operations, generating **$787.55K** (over 65% of net sales), despite keeping a closely identical average ticket size (~$141) with baseline grocery stores.
* ** Grocery Stores** exhibit the lowest inventory allocation (1,083 items) but maintain steady average pricing ($140.29).

### 2. High-Margin Inventory Categories
* ** Fruits and Vegetables ($178.12K)** and **Snack Foods ($175.43K)** represent the highest-grossing inventory item blocks.
* ** Seafood ($9.08K)** reflects the lowest operational volume, indicating potential supply-chain constraints or lower localized demand.

### 3. Demographic & Location Allocation
* ** Tier 3 cities** generated the highest geographic revenue segment at **$472.13K**, outperforming Tier 1 ($336.40K) and Tier 2 ($393.15K) setups.
* ** Medium-sized outlets** capture the highest operational share, bringing in **$507.90K (42.27%)** of gross transactional throughput.
